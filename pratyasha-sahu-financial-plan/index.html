Do Not Buy

Avoid ULIPs, endowment plans, F&O, crypto, sector funds, stock tips, and investing wedding money in equity. Limit Shriram or other NBFC FD exposure because it is not the same safety category as a bank FD.

Market Fall Rule

If equity mutual funds fall 20-30%, do not sell long-term SIPs. Continue SIPs. The protection happens before the fall by keeping 0-3 year money outside equity.
